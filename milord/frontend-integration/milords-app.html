01Wake a Milord

You lock1,000,000$MILORD, held in escrow
Plus an ETH fee—10% of the ETH value of 1M $MILORD, min 0.002 ETH
On redeem900,000back to you, 100,000 burned

The fee comes from a short-lived signed quote priced from the pool's 30-minute average, never its instant price. Right after launch, or if pricing is unavailable, waking waits; redeeming never does. Your Milord's ID comes from the returned pool first, then the next new one. Fees are split 70% to arbitrage funding and 30% to the team.

03Rewards

Claimable pARB—

Protocol fees are swapped to USDG and deposited into the pARBITRAGE vault on Robinhood Chain; the shares go to Milords held during each UTC week, weighted by time held. Earlier holders keep what they earned. pARB has a 0% performance fee here, its price can fall, and redemption goes through the vault's queue. Nothing here is a promise of return.

04Governance

Your votes—

Each awake Milord is one vote once you activate (self-delegate). Governance is advisory in V1: votes signal, the Team Safe executes.
