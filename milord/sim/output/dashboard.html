MILORD local simulation

Local single-chain simulation (as on Robinhood Chain) with hypothetical SCENARIO inputs and mock USDG, swap and quote price. Not performance data, not a forecast.

Three UTC weeks on one local chain configured like Robinhood Chain (token, NFTs, pARB vault and distributor together, no bridge), against the real contracts. Generated 2026-10-13T18:46:01.028Z. Start: 0 Milords, no holders.

Scenario inputs (hypothetical)

  • weiPerMillionEarly: 0.03 ETH
  • weiPerMillionLater: 0.012 ETH
  • ethUsd: $2500 per ETH (mock ETH→USDG swap)
  • royaltySaleEth: 0.1 ETH
  • tradingTaxEthWeek1: 0.15 ETH
  • pnlBpsWeek2: 2%
  • pnlBpsWeek3: -3%

What happened each week

  • week 1: first 100 Milords; one royalty-paying sale, one without; illustrative tax inflow
  • week 2: one redemption (day 1.5, reissued later from inventory), one sale mid-week (day 3.5), 10 new conversions, +2% scenario P&L
  • week 3: -3% scenario P&L; 5 new conversions; characteristic 9 and strategy 2 added; one holder selected the new pair
