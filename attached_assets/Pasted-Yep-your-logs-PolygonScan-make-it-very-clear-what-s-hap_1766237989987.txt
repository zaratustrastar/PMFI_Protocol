Yep — your logs + PolygonScan make it very clear what’s happening:

### the tx is failing because the Safe proxy doesn’t actually have 19.99 USDC.e

On the proxy address **0x9cbc…**, PolygonScan shows the USDC.e balance is **19.989265** (not 19.99). ([Polygon (POL) Blockchain Explorer][1])

But your script tries to transfer exactly **19.99** (i.e. **19,990,000** in 6-decimals). From the tx input it’s calling `transfer(EOA, 0x013105f0)` where `0x013105f0 = 19,990,000`. ([Polygon (POL) Blockchain Explorer][2])

Since **19,989,265 < 19,990,000**, the USDC `transfer()` fails, which makes the Safe `execTransaction()` revert. That’s why there are **no ERC-20 transfer logs** and the tx is “execution reverted”. ([Polygon (POL) Blockchain Explorer][2])

This is a classic “UI rounding” bug:

* your bot prints “Proxy USDC.e balance: $19.99” but it likely rounded up
* the actual balance has a tiny shortfall (~$0.000735)

## how to fix it (MVP-safe)

### 1) never transfer the human amount directly

Convert using exact integer math and cap by balance:

* read `balanceRaw` (integer, 6 decimals)
* read `requestedRaw = floor(requested * 1e6)`
* set `sendRaw = min(requestedRaw, balanceRaw)`

### 2) optionally keep a tiny dust buffer

To avoid edge weirdness:

* `sendRaw = min(requestedRaw, balanceRaw - 100)` (i.e. keep 0.0001 USDC)

### 3) show the exact amount you’ll send

Log both:

* requested
* available
* final send amount

So you’ll see immediately when it’s being capped.
