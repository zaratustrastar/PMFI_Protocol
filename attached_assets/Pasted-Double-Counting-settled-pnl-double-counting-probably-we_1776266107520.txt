Double Counting

settled_pnl double-counting? probably we do not need it all since we will count one leg  at 99c and other at 0c. 

Looking at where settled_pnl appears:

In arb_nav.py:

python

settled_pnl = _get_settled_pnl()  # SUM(settled_pnl_usdc) WHERE status='settled'
total_assets = poly_cash + kalshi_cash + opinion_cash + servicer_on_base + total_liquid + settled_pnl

And in arb_reporter.py:

python

settled_pnl = _get_settled_pnl()
raw_total = vault_idle + servicer_on_base + poly_cash + kalshi_cash + opinion_cash + settled_pnl

The double-counting risk: When a position is settled, the winnings are claimed from the platform and become platform cash (poly_cash, kalshi_cash, or opinion_cash). If the position is then marked status='settled' with settled_pnl_usdc = profit, that profit is counted both as platform cash AND as settled_pnl.

Example: A position settles for $1.00 per share, cost basis was $0.95, profit = $0.05. After claiming, Polymarket balance increases by $1.00 (full claim amount). settled_pnl_usdc records $0.05. But poly_cash already includes the full $1.00 of claimed funds. So the $0.05 is double-counted.

Actually, looking more carefully: settled_pnl_usdc likely records the profit (claim - cost basis), not the full claim amount. But the full claim amount is already in platform cash. So the NAV becomes: poly_cash (includes $1.00 claim) + settled_pnl ($0.05 profit) = $1.05, when the real value is $1.00. The cost basis ($0.95) was already counted when the position was opened (it reduced servicer/platform cash by $0.95). So after settlement, the net position should just be the $1.00 claim in platform cash — no separate settled_pnl addition.

This is a confirmed double-counting bug. The fix: either (a) exclude settled_pnl from the NAV/reporter sum entirely (since claimed funds are already in platform cash), or (b) subtract cost_basis from settled positions to avoid counting the original investment twice — but this is complex. The cleanest fix is (a): remove settled_pnl from the sum, since claimed funds flow into platform balances automatically.

Other potential double-counting but probably it won't happen because reporter function executes every hour: servicer_on_base + poly_cash could overlap if funds were just sent from servicer to Polymarket but haven't been deducted from the servicer balance yet (pending Base transaction).
