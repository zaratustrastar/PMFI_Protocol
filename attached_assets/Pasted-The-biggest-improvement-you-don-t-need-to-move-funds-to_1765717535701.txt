The biggest improvement: you don’t need to move funds to your EOA first

Your flow currently says:

Safe → transfer USDC.e → EOA (Polygon) → Relay bridge → Base

That works, but it’s one extra hop and increases operational risk (EOA temporarily holds more funds; more approvals; more code).

Better MVP flow (fewer steps)

Safe → bridge contract (Polygon) → Base treasury → vault

Meaning: the Safe executes a transfer directly to the Relay bridge “sender” address/contract (or whatever Relay expects), instead of first transferring to your EOA.

Only do the EOA hop if Relay requires the sender to be an EOA (some bridges do, some don’t).

Critical checks before you ship this
1) Confirm the Safe is actually controllable by your EOA

Polymarket supports multiple “signature types” (EOA vs proxy vs Gnosis Safe). Your account type matters.

You want to verify on-chain:

getOwners() contains your EOA

getThreshold() == 1

If it’s threshold 1 and you’re the only owner, Protocol Kit will be straightforward.

2) Gas reality

Your message says:

“Your EOA pays gas (~$0.01 MATIC) for the Safe tx”

Correct: the executor pays gas, and in your setup that will be your keeper EOA submitting the Safe execution tx on Polygon. (Polymarket’s relayer infra is mainly for gasless trading flows; don’t assume it will cover your custom transfer out.)

So yes: fund the keeper EOA with MATIC.

3) USDC.e vs USDC

Your Safe likely holds USDC.e on Polygon (common Polymarket collateral). Make sure:

you transfer the right token

your bridge expects that token and outputs what you want on Base

MVP security guardrails I strongly recommend

Because once you automate “Safe can send tokens anywhere,” the main risk becomes your keeper getting compromised.

Hard-allowlist destination addresses

only allow transfers to:

your Base treasury (via bridge)

your vault

maybe one cold wallet (emergency)

Cap outflow per hour/day

e.g. max $5k/hour or whatever your MVP caps imply

Dry-run simulation before sending

simulate the Safe exec call / estimate gas

abort if it would fail (wrong nonce, insufficient balance, token address mismatch)

Separate keys

oracle signer key must never be the same as the keeper executor key
