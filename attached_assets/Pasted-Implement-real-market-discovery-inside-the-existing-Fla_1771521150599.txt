B) Opinion adapter (bot/arb_monitor/adapters/opinion.py)

  1) sortBy=ending soon
  2) sortBy=volume7d desc (or the closest supported)
- Pagination budget: ARB_MAX_PAGES_OPINION (default 10)
- For each market list item, extract:
  marketId, marketTitle, createdAt, cutoffAt (expiryTs), and token ids (yesTokenId, noTokenId).
  If token ids are missing in list response, implement a minimal per-market detail fetch:
    GET {OPINION_BASE_URL}/market/{marketId}
  Cache market details for 30 minutes to keep requests minimal.
