Before you deposit

This is a custodial, manually settled migration, not an automatic swap. Tokens go to a project-controlled multisig and transfers are not automatically reversible.

Your final ratio depends on completed sales, purchases, fees, and execution prices. No value or exchange rate is guaranteed.
